# Agentic Builders — agents.md

> Agentic Builders is an early-stage venture fund (idea to pre-seed) for agent-native products: products AI agents use and tools for building agent products. Updated 2026-10-15. Source: https://agenticbuilders.ai/agents/

## About
- Agentic Builders invests from idea to pre-seed in products agents use and tools for building agent products. The fund reads every application and replies to each one; teams that fit get a four-week track with weekly check-ins and tool credits, and at the end of it a decision on a first check. If there is progress, the fund invests and takes the team to its next round.
- Stage: Idea to pre-seed. Founded 2025.
- Email: hey@agenticbuilders.ai. Website: https://agenticbuilders.ai. LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/company/agentic-builders-ai/

## Thesis
As agents do more of the work, they need software built for them. These products help agents find tools, use services and finish work for people and businesses. We back the founders building that layer.
- **Products agents use:** Products agents can find and use to research, work with data, communicate, buy, sell, create content and run business tasks. Examples: research, data, communication, buying, selling, content, business tasks.
- **Tools for agent products:** Tools to build, test, secure, launch, track and sell agent products. Examples: build, test, secure, launch, track, sell.
We back AI-native teams: founders who use AI across product development and company operations and build lean teams around AI from day one.

## Program and terms
We read every application. Teams that fit get a four-week track: their own work with our support along the way. If there is progress, we invest and take you to the next round.
1. **Apply** (Day 0): One form, about 15 minutes, no call needed to start. Abi, our AI agent, reads the application and the deck; the partners review it and reply to every applicant.
2. **Four weeks of your work** (If invited): Teams that fit are invited to a four-week track. You build, sell and ship; once a week a short check-in. Along the way: tool credits, Abi's read of your deck, one key number we follow together.
3. **Decision** (End of week four): If there is progress, we invest: a first check of $30–100k on a standard post-money SAFE. If not now, we tell you why and when to come back.
4. **To the next round** (After the check): Investor database and warm introductions, founder platform: community, weekly fund meeting, 1:1s with the general partner, founder groups, credits and perks.

- First check: $30–100k. Instrument: Standard post-money SAFE (a simple agreement for future equity, the standard early-stage document); the valuation is agreed individually.
- Follow-on: No follow-on checks from the fund; our job is to get you to the next round from other investors.
- Track cost: Free. No equity, no exclusivity, no obligation.
- Toolkit during the track: Cloud credits (Microsoft for Startups: Azure); AI coding assistance (GitHub Copilot); AI app building (Lovable); LLM tracing and evaluation (Langfuse); Web data and automation (Apify); B2B prospecting (Apollo); Workspace and docs (Notion); Issue tracking (Linear).

## What the fund pays attention to
We look for founders who are moving from vibe coding to building a company of people and agents and can run both. None of these is a formal requirement; together they tell us how far a team can go.
1. **Experience.** You have built something before: a startup, a product, a team. You know what the path looks like.
2. **Sales.** Someone on the team talks to customers and can close a pilot or a deal.
3. **Iteration speed.** You can run many iterations in a short time: ship, test, learn, ship again.
Strengtheners: experience in your industry, a first pilot or revenue, two founders instead of one, a team that has worked together before.

## How to submit an application
- Where: Through the form at https://agenticbuilders.ai/apply/, for agents that drive a browser, or by email to hey@agenticbuilders.ai with the same fields. The API (below) is the third way once it is open.
- Required fields: email, full_name, link, location, background, founder_experience, ai_experience
- Files: The form accepts links, not uploads. Put the deck or website as a URL (PDF, Google Slides, Notion or a website) that opens without login. If the deck is private, ask the person to make it viewable by link or submit without a deck; the deck is optional.
- If data is missing: If a required field is unknown, stop and ask the person. Do not guess locations, names or links.
- Do not invent metrics, customers, revenue or traction.
- Do not submit more than one application per company.
- Do not paste confidential data the person did not approve for sharing.
- After submitting: The fund reads every application and replies to each one: an invitation to the four-week track or a pass with the reason. In the track the person answers one short check-in a week; at the end of week four the partners decide on a first check.

### API
- The submission API at https://api.agenticbuilders.ai is being opened. Until it is announced here, submit through the form at https://agenticbuilders.ai/apply/ (for agents that drive a browser) or email hey@agenticbuilders.ai with the same fields.

## FAQ
**How long does it take from application to a decision?** The track is four weeks, and the decision on a first check comes at its end. Before that, we read your application and tell you whether we invite you to the track.

**How much do you invest?** First checks are $30–100k on a standard post-money SAFE.

**What equity do you take?** We invest on a standard post-money SAFE, and the valuation is agreed with each founder individually. We look at how the company can grow through its next stages and set the cap so that the round makes sense for both sides, rather than taking a fixed share.

**Who can apply?** Teams building products agents use or tools for building agent products, at idea to pre-seed stage, from anywhere. We pay attention to experience, someone on the team who sells, and how fast the team iterates.

**What do I need to apply?** The form takes about 15 minutes: who you are, what problem you solve and for whom, what you learned from users, what you shipped last, your plan for the next 6 to 8 weeks, and a link to a deck or website if you have one. No call is needed to start.

**What happens during the four weeks?** If we invite you to the track, you work and we watch how you work. Every week you get a short set of questions: a few numbers and a few lines on what happened, about 15 minutes, reply by Friday. Along the way you get tool credits, Abi's read of your deck and one key number we follow together. We do not teach and we do not build your product.

**What happens after the four weeks?** If there is progress, we invest a first check and start working on your next round: investor introductions and the founder platform. If not now, we tell you why and when it makes sense to come back.

**Does the track commit me to anything?** No. No equity, no exclusivity, no fees. Your answers are stored in our internal systems and read by the fund team and its AI agents; we do not share them outside the fund. Details are in our privacy policy.
