How we work.

Thesis
Agent-native products: products agents use and tools for building agent products.
Stage
Idea to pre-seed.
Program
We read every application. Teams that fit get a four-week track with weekly check-ins and tool credits, and a decision on a first check at its end. If there is progress we invest and work on the next round with the team: investor introductions and the founder platform.
Selection
Three things we pay attention to: experience, someone on the team who sells, and iteration speed.
How we run
Abi, the fund's AI agent, handles intake and deck analysis, weekly check-ins, investment memos and portfolio tracking. Partners decide; Abi prepares.
