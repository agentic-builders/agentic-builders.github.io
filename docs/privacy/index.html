Privacy policy.

Last updated 2026-10-15.

Who we are

Agentic Builders Capital LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, operates agenticbuilders.ai and the founder program described on it and makes the investments. Contact: hey@agenticbuilders.ai.

What we collect

  • Applications: name, email, links you provide (LinkedIn, website, deck), location, the answers in the form, and any documents you link to.
  • Track check-ins: the numbers and answers you send during the four-week track, including text, voice messages, screenshots and files.
  • Meetings: name, organization, email and notes.
  • Newsletter: email address and whether you opened or clicked a letter.
  • Website: we do not use analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

How we use it

  • To review applications, run the track, decide on investments and keep in touch with you about them.
  • To organize meetings and to send the newsletter you subscribed to.
  • To improve our selection process, using aggregated information.

AI processing

Applications, decks and check-in answers are read and summarized by software agents run by the fund. These agents use large language models provided by third parties under contracts that do not permit the provider to train on your data. People make every decision; the agents prepare.

Who sees it

The fund team and its agents. Service providers that host our systems (cloud hosting, database, workspace and email tools, model providers) process data on our behalf. We do not sell personal data. We share information about a company with investors only with the founders' agreement, for example in an introduction they asked for.

Legal basis (EU/UK)

Performance of steps you ask for before a possible contract (your application and the track), our legitimate interest in running a venture fund, and your consent for the newsletter, which you can withdraw at any time.

Retention

Applications and track data: five years from the last contact, so that we can reconsider a company later and meet our record-keeping obligations. Newsletter data: until you unsubscribe. You can ask us to delete your data earlier.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, as provided by the law that applies to you (including GDPR, UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act). Write to hey@agenticbuilders.ai. EU and UK residents may complain to their supervisory authority.

International transfers

We are based in the US. Data from the EU and UK is transferred under standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date.
