Our portfolio

Meet the companies we back: products AI agents use and tools for building agent products.

For angels & VCs
  • EqvestaA digital family office.

    Developing AI agents that bring accounts across countries and currencies into one view, track filing deadlines and prepare documents for a CPA. It does not hold or move money.

  • SilowPrioritized plans for AI adoption.

    Maps how a team works across its existing tools and ranks automation opportunities by expected payback. Companies get an AI roadmap they own, with recommendations on what to automate and how to measure the results.

  • HeronAgent governance.

    Maps a company’s agents, processes and ownership. When people, agents or workflows change, it identifies what is affected and prepares updates for a person to approve.

  • koneAI adoption, apps and training.

    Helps companies adopt AI tools, automate internal processes and build tailored applications. Provides training and implementation support for their teams.

  • AgentiqaRelease checks for production agents.

    Checks what AI agents write to business systems such as ERP, CRM and billing. Tests data accuracy, retries and misuse before release, blocking releases when checks fail.

  • TheonaAgents for team workflows.

    Maps how teams work across conversations, meetings and task trackers, then builds agents into existing tools such as Slack, Teams and CRM systems. Agents start in draft mode for human review.

  • HookRadarAgentic creative director.

    Finds creative patterns in live ads, organic content and competitor activity. Turns those signals into prioritized ideas and briefs that marketing teams can use to plan their next tests.

  • VisibilityProVisibility in AI search.

    Combines technical website improvements, content production and external mentions to help brands appear in AI answers. Uses AI agents with human review and measures mentions, citations and referral traffic.

  • Aprena AcademyCareer planning for experienced professionals.

    Helps experienced professionals explore realistic career paths using their existing skills, job market data and human support. Identifies skill gaps and offers practical learning and regular progress updates.

  • FinaAutomation for finance teams.

    Processes invoices and bank statements, matches transactions with documents and prepares financial reports. Delivers files for finance teams to review and upload to their accounting systems.
