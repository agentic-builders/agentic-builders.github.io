Software built for AI agents

As agents do more of the work, they need software built for them. These products help agents find tools, use services and complete tasks for people and businesses. We back the founders building these products.

We back products AI agents use and tools for building agent products.

Who we back

We back founders who build lean teams of people and AI agents, using agents across product development and day-to-day operations.

Three things we look for:

  1. 1Experience. You have built a startup, product or team before, and know what it takes.
  2. 2Founder-led sales. Founders talk directly to customers and can secure a pilot or close a sale.
  3. 3Iteration speed. You ship, test, learn and improve quickly.

None of these is a formal requirement; together, they help us assess a team's potential.

How the investment track works

We review every application. If invited, join our investment track: a fully online, four-week program where you keep building your business with our support, wherever you're based. At the end, the fund’s investment partners decide whether to invest.

  1. 1

    Four weeks of your work

    If invited, you keep building, selling and shipping with our support. Each week, send a short update by Friday: a few key numbers and a brief account of your progress. It takes about 15 minutes.

    • Software credits and discounts to help cover the cost of cloud services and AI tokens
    • Abi, the fund's AI agent, reads your pitch deck and gives back the questions investors will ask
    • Help choosing and tracking one measure of your business’s progress
  2. 2

    An investment decision

    At the end of the four weeks, the fund’s investment partners decide whether to invest and explain their decision. If we do not invest, we explain when it makes sense to come back.

    • Investment of $30–100k on a standard post-money SAFE
    • Valuation agreed with you, not a fixed share
    • Participation in the investment track is free, with no equity requirement or exclusivity
  3. 3

    Help with your next round

    After investing, we help you raise your next funding round. We do not invest additional money in later rounds; we help you connect with other investors.

    • A list of relevant investors and personal introductions to investors who fund your next stage
    • A community of founders and weekly meetings with the fund team
    • One-on-one sessions with Nikolay Vyahhi, the fund’s general partner, and founder discussion groups on building AI agents, product development and sales
    • Additional software credits and discounts

Every application gets a reply. Application review has no fixed turnaround time; the four-week investment track begins only after an invitation. During the investment track, we ask for a short weekly progress update. Participation is free and requires no equity or exclusivity. Any investment is agreed separately.

Who you work with

Our investment and operations team supports founders from application through their next funding round. The fund’s investment partners make the investment decisions; Abi, the fund’s AI agent, helps review applications and prepare investment summaries.

Questions founders ask

How long does it take from application to a decision?

Application review has no fixed turnaround time. If invited, you enter a four-week investment track, with an investment decision at its end.

How much do you invest?

We invest $30–100k on a standard post-money SAFE.

What equity do you take?

We invest on a standard post-money SAFE, and the valuation is agreed with each founder individually. We look at how the company can grow through its next stages and set the valuation cap so that the round makes sense for both sides, rather than taking a fixed share.

Who can apply?

Founders can apply from anywhere in the world, including before incorporating. We invest only in US-incorporated companies, so a US company must be in place before an investment is completed. We back teams building products AI agents use or tools for building agent products, from idea to pre-seed. We consider experience, founder-led sales and speed of execution.

What do I need to apply?

The form takes about 15 minutes: who you are, what problem you solve and for whom, what you learned from users, what you shipped last, your plan for the next 6 to 8 weeks, and a link to a pitch deck or website if you have one. No call is needed to start.

What happens during the four weeks?

The four-week investment track is fully online. You keep building your business with our support. Each week, send a short update by Friday: a few key numbers and a brief account of your progress. It takes about 15 minutes. You get software credits and discounts, feedback on your pitch deck from Abi, the fund’s AI agent, and help tracking one measure of your business’s progress. You remain responsible for building your product.

What happens after the four weeks?

The fund’s investment partners decide whether to invest and explain their decision. If we invest, we provide $30–100k and help you prepare for your next round through investor introductions and founder support. If we do not invest, we explain when it makes sense to come back.

Does the investment track commit me to anything?

Participation is free and requires no equity or exclusivity. During the investment track, we ask for a short weekly progress update. There is no obligation to accept an investment; any investment is agreed separately.

Who can access my application?

Your answers are stored in our internal systems and read by the fund team and its AI agents. Service providers process data on our behalf. We share information with investors only with your consent. Details are in our privacy policy.
