As agents do more of the work, they need software built for them. These products help agents find tools, use services and finish work for people and businesses. We back the founders building that layer.

We back two kinds of products. Products agents useresearchdatabuysell, and tools for agent productsbuildtestsecurelaunch.

We look for founders who are moving from vibe coding to building a company of people and agents and can run both. Three things we pay attention to:

  1. 1Experience. You have built something before: a startup, a product, a team. You know what the path looks like.
  2. 2Sales. Someone on the team talks to customers and can close a pilot or a deal.
  3. 3Iteration speed. You can run many iterations in a short time: ship, test, learn, ship again.

None of these is a formal requirement; together they tell us how far a team can go.

How it works. We read every application. Teams that fit get a four-week track: their own work with our support along the way. If there is progress, we invest and take you to the next round.

  1. 1

    Four weeks of your work

    We read every application and reply to each one. If your team fits, we invite you to a four-week track. You build, sell and ship; we watch how you work. Once a week, a short check-in: a few numbers and a few lines on what happened, about 15 minutes, reply by Friday.

    • Tool credits: cloud, AI coding and app building, LLM tracing, web data, prospecting, docs
    • Abi, our AI agent, reads your deck and gives you back the questions investors will ask
    • One key number we follow together
  2. 2

    If there is progress, we invest

    At the end of the four weeks the partners decide on a first check and tell you why, either way. If not now, we say when it makes sense to come back.

    • First check of $30–100k on a standard post-money SAFE
    • Valuation agreed with you, not a fixed share
    • No fees, no exclusivity for the four weeks
  3. 3

    Then we get you to the round

    We do not do follow-on checks. Our job is to make sure the next money comes from other investors, and we work on that with you.

    • Curated investor database and warm introductions to the investors who do the next round
    • Founder platform: community, weekly fund meeting, 1:1s with the general partner, founder groups on AI engineering, product and sales, a wider set of credits and perks

Every application gets a reply. In the track, the only thing we ask for is a weekly check-in. Free. No equity, no exclusivity, no obligation.

Questions founders ask.

How long does it take from application to a decision?

The track is four weeks, and the decision on a first check comes at its end. Before that, we read your application and tell you whether we invite you to the track.

How much do you invest?

First checks are $30–100k on a standard post-money SAFE.

What equity do you take?

We invest on a standard post-money SAFE, and the valuation is agreed with each founder individually. We look at how the company can grow through its next stages and set the cap so that the round makes sense for both sides, rather than taking a fixed share.

Who can apply?

Teams building products agents use or tools for building agent products, at idea to pre-seed stage, from anywhere. We pay attention to experience, someone on the team who sells, and how fast the team iterates.

What do I need to apply?

The form takes about 15 minutes: who you are, what problem you solve and for whom, what you learned from users, what you shipped last, your plan for the next 6 to 8 weeks, and a link to a deck or website if you have one. No call is needed to start.

What happens during the four weeks?

If we invite you to the track, you work and we watch how you work. Every week you get a short set of questions: a few numbers and a few lines on what happened, about 15 minutes, reply by Friday. Along the way you get tool credits, Abi's read of your deck and one key number we follow together. We do not teach and we do not build your product.

What happens after the four weeks?

If there is progress, we invest a first check and start working on your next round: investor introductions and the founder platform. If not now, we tell you why and when it makes sense to come back.

Does the track commit me to anything?

No. No equity, no exclusivity, no fees. Your answers are stored in our internal systems and read by the fund team and its AI agents; we do not share them outside the fund. Details are in our privacy policy.

One form. Four weeks. A decision either way.
