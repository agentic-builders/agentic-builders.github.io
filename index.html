Who We Back

Five things we look for in every team. Missing one usually means it is not a fit yet.

What strengthens an application: experience in your industry, a first pilot or revenue, two founders instead of one, and a team that has worked together before.

  1. Built a startup before. Not necessarily a successful one. You know what the path looks like.
  2. Sell into the US. Your target market is the US, or global with first customers in the US.
  3. Someone on the team sells. At least one customer or pilot closed personally, in any project.
  4. Can be in the US. A passport or visa, weeks on the ground, and a move when the round comes.
  5. Share numbers monthly. Seven fields, once a month, without reminders, and show up to the cohort rhythm.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Agentic Builders invest in?

Agentic Builders invests in agent-native applications: products that AI agents find and use to research, work with data, communicate, buy, sell, create content and run business tasks, and tools to build, test, secure, launch, track and sell agent products.

What stage and check size does Agentic Builders invest at?

Agentic Builders invests from idea to pre-seed. A first check is $30,000 to $80,000 on a standard post-money SAFE. The fund does not do follow-on checks; its job is to get the team to the next round from other investors.

What is an agent-native application?

An agent-native application is software designed for AI agents as its primary users, not people. Agents discover it, call it and pay for it to complete work on behalf of people and businesses. Examples include data services, payment and monetization layers, testing and audit tools, and agent platforms.

Who does Agentic Builders back?

AI-native teams that use AI across product development and company operations. We look for founders who have built a startup before, sell into the US market, have someone on the team who sells, can physically be in the US, and share numbers monthly.

Does the fund build the product for the team?

No. The founder builds the product. Agentic Builders provides the first check, a cohort of peer founders, guidance on AI and product architecture, help with legal, incorporation and operations, and introductions to the next investors when the company is ready to raise.

How does the cohort work?

Teams join in cohorts with a shared start date, a shared weekly rhythm, shared go-to-market materials, monthly updates and a demo day where the cohort is shown to friendly funds. Founders also get guild sessions on AI engineering, product and go-to-market, plus tool credits and discounts.

How do I apply to Agentic Builders?

Fill in the application form or email hey@agenticbuilders.ai. Applications are reviewed weekly. Include what you are building, who the agent user is, your team and any early customers or pilots.
